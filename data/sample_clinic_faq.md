# Apex Health Clinic — Receptionist Knowledge Base

## 1. General Information & Operating Hours
* **Clinic Name:** Apex Health Clinic
* **Address:** 742 Evergreen Terrace, Suite 100, Springfield
* **Operating Hours:**
  * Monday through Friday: 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM
  * Saturday: 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM
  * Sunday: Closed (Emergency on-call physician available via dispatch)
* **Main Telephone:** (555) 019-2834
* **Emergency Policy:** If a caller is experiencing chest pain, severe bleeding, or shortness of breath, immediately instruct them to hang up and dial 911 or visit the nearest emergency room.

## 2. Appointments & Scheduling
* **Booking an Appointment:** Patients can book appointments online at apexhealth.example.com or by calling during office hours.
* **Cancellation Policy:** Appointments must be canceled or rescheduled at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a $35 late cancellation fee.
* **Walk-Ins:** Walk-in patients are accepted for urgent minor illnesses between 9:00 AM and 1:00 PM on weekdays, subject to availability.
* **Telehealth Consultations:** Telehealth video appointments are available Monday through Thursday for non-emergency follow-ups.

## 3. Accepted Insurance & Payment Methods
* **Accepted Insurance Plans:** BlueCross BlueShield, Aetna, Medicare Part B, UnitedHealthcare, and Cigna.
* **Unaccepted Plans:** Medicaid HMO plans are currently not accepted.
* **Self-Pay Discount:** Uninsured patients receive a 20% discount if paid in full on the date of service.
* **Accepted Payment Options:** Credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), debit cards, HSA/FSA cards, and cash.

## 4. Prescription Refills
* Prescription refill requests require 48 hours notice.
* Refill requests should be submitted directly through your pharmacy or via the patient portal.
* Controlled substance refills require an in-person office visit every 90 days.
